# Domain Guidance

Read only the relevant domain. Use domain guidance to change composition, evidence, density, language, and interaction attitude, not merely colors and nouns.

## Enterprise AI and Developer Tools

Primary jobs: authenticate, configure, inspect runs, manage models/data/agents, debug, deploy, monitor, control access.

Evidence: real workflow, API/code truth, model/version metadata, permissions, traces, evaluations, datasets, deployment state.

Avoid: generic neural networks, particles, glowing orbits, fake terminals, invented metrics, abstract dashboards, "ship faster" copy.

## Security and Regulated Operations

Primary jobs: verify identity, select scope, understand authorization state, recover access, acknowledge or escalate.

Evidence: device/session state, operator role, policy, facility/tenant scope, audit and recovery paths.

Use explicit labels such as `MFA REQUIRED`, `设备未登记`, `访问级别不足`, `会话已锁定` where appropriate. Keep motion restrained and consequence clear.

## Dashboards and Internal Tools

Prioritize scan speed, comparison, filters, selection, bulk actions, state, and repeated operation. Start with the working surface, not a marketing hero. Use tables when column comparison matters, lists for scanning, and cards for object inspection.

## Finance, Insurance, and Legal

Prioritize trust, provenance, risk disclosure, document clarity, confirmation, and consequences. Do not use fake precision or decorative charts. Numbers require timeframe and source.

## Healthcare and Life Sciences

Prioritize safety, privacy, evidence, clinical workflow, patient/sample context, and conservative motion. State and warnings must not rely on color alone.

## Government and Public Service

Prioritize task completion, familiar patterns, accessibility, eligibility, status, and plain language. Brand presence should not bury the service.

## Education and Learning

Prioritize learning path, progress, examples, feedback, instructor/institution credibility, and accessible reading. Show what the learner does and how progress is understood.

## Publishing, Research, and Editorial

Prioritize catalog/search, real publication objects, authors/editors, dates, DOI/ISBN where real, review/submission processes, citations, and readable editorial rhythm. Avoid fake covers, faux heritage palettes, or icon grids as proof.

## Commerce

Prioritize product inspection, price, variants, availability, delivery/returns, trust, and purchase path. Real product imagery is required. Atmosphere cannot replace inspection.

## Culture, Portfolio, and Entertainment

Allow stronger art direction and motion, but ground it in real work, media, people, places, or gameplay. A memorable scene must still reveal identity and navigation.
